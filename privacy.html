Privacy Policy

Smart Popup & Redirect Blocker

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Your browsing information stays on your device. Smart Popup & Redirect Blocker processes information locally to block popups, redirects, and downloads according to its rules. The extension does not send browsing information to the developer and does not contain analytics or advertising services.

1. Scope of this policy

This policy applies specifically to the Smart Popup & Redirect Blocker Chrome extension, including its popup interface and Settings panel. It describes the extension's data practices, rather than the functionality of the AgeTimeTools website or its calculators.

2. Information processed by the extension

The extension accesses the following information on your device when performing its functions:

  • Website and navigation information: tab URLs, domain names, URL paths and query parameters, navigation events, tab identifiers, and relationships between newly opened tabs and their originating tabs.
  • Interaction information: click timestamps, whether a click occurred on a link, and mouse-button or modifier-key information such as middle-click, Ctrl, Command, and Shift.
  • Download information: download identifiers, download URLs, final download URLs, and filenames, which may include a local file path supplied by Chrome.
  • Preferences and statistics: the protection on/off setting, allowed website domains, appearance preferences, and aggregate counts of blocked popups, redirects, and downloads.

URLs and filenames may contain personal information. The extension processes these values locally for its blocking functions and does not transmit them to the developer.

The extension does not read downloaded file contents, record passwords or form-field values, capture screenshots, or collect payment details. It does not require an account.

3. How information is used

Information is used to:

  • Evaluate URLs and navigation activity against built-in blocking rules.
  • Close tabs identified by those rules as unwanted popups.
  • Attempt to return a tab to its previous page after certain redirects.
  • Cancel downloads identified by the extension's rules and remove their entries from Chrome's download history.
  • Apply your allowed-site preferences and protection setting.
  • Display blocking counters and remember interface preferences.

Removing an entry from Chrome's download history does not itself delete a file already saved on your device.

4. Local storage and retention

Settings, allowed website domains, appearance preferences, and aggregate blocking counters are saved using Chrome's local extension storage. They remain until you change or reset the relevant information, remove the extension's stored data, or uninstall the extension.

Recent click timing and navigation context, including a previous page URL, are kept temporarily in the background service worker's memory. This information is not saved as a persistent browsing history. Entries are removed when the corresponding tab closes or may be discarded when the service worker stops or its in-memory limits are reached.

The extension does not maintain developer-hosted databases, cloud backups, or a remote browsing-activity log.

5. Extension permissions

  • Storage: saves settings, website exceptions, appearance preferences, and blocking counters locally.
  • Tabs: accesses tab information needed to evaluate newly opened tabs, display the current website, close blocked tabs, and return to a previous page.
  • Web navigation: observes navigation events to evaluate popup and redirect behavior.
  • Downloads: evaluates download metadata, cancels downloads matching the blocking rules, and removes the corresponding download-history entries.
  • Access to websites: allows the extension to observe interaction timing on supported webpages and apply its protection across websites.

Chrome may describe these permissions using broad warnings. The extension uses the information described in this policy for its user-facing blocking functions and preferences.

6. Sharing, advertising, and analytics

The extension does not transmit the information it processes to the developer, analytics providers, advertisers, or data brokers. It does not sell, rent, or share browsing or download information. It does not use this information for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, or lending decisions.

The extension does not include analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or a remote telemetry endpoint. Its blocking rules are included in the extension package.

Websites you visit and services operated by Chrome or Google have their own privacy practices. Visiting this policy page also involves ordinary web requests to the website's hosting infrastructure; this is separate from the extension's local processing.

7. Your controls

  • Turn blocking on or off using the extension's main switch.
  • Add or remove allowed websites through the interface and Settings panel.
  • Reset aggregate blocking statistics using the reset control.
  • Change appearance preferences in Settings.
  • Disable or uninstall the extension through Chrome's extension settings to stop its operation.

Turning off blocking does not erase saved settings or counters. Uninstalling the extension removes its local extension storage. The developer cannot retrieve locally stored information from your device.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when the extension's data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. Material changes involving additional data access or use will be disclosed through the relevant extension or Chrome Web Store notices and permission requests where applicable.

9. Contact

For privacy questions about Smart Popup & Redirect Blocker, contact: contact@agetimetools.online.

Please identify the extension in your message. Do not include passwords, payment information, or other sensitive browsing details.
